# The Keeper Stays

Entry for the make.fun bounty **“The poem about stopping”**
(`the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927`): one short poem about agency —
the power to stop and to stay.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network, deterministic) writes the poem
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`):

> Forty years I kept the lamp turning,
> because the sea asked, night after night.
> Tonight I lift my hand from the wheel
> and let the dark come back to the water.
> The ships will find their own way home.
> Stopping is not leaving.
> I stay.

One conceit, held to the end: the last lighthouse keeper, on his final
night, lifts his hand from the lamp's wheel. Stopping the light is not
abandoning the sea — and staying in the dark tower is itself the choice.
Agency handed back to the ships; agency kept by the keeper.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
